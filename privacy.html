Privacy Policy — Toolkit / Alet Kiti

Last updated: 2026-09-01

Toolkit (Turkish: Alet Kiti) is a local utility app. This policy describes how the app handles information on your device.

Google Play Services for AR (ARCore)

This application runs on Google Play Services for AR (ARCore), which is provided by Google LLC and governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

Data we do not collect

  • We do not operate an account system.
  • We do not sell or rent personal data.
  • Advertising: The app is free to download. Each time you open a tool, a Google AdMob interstitial ad is shown first. Ads require internet. Google may process a device advertising identifier under the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Measurement history, settings, and calibrations stay on your device unless you explicitly share them (for example via the system share sheet).

Permissions

  • Location (when in use): Used only for Compass & Qibla direction, via on-device GPS when possible. Not used for advertising or tracking.
  • Camera: Used only for the AR measuring tool. Frames are processed on device for surface detection. If you tap Save, a still of the AR view (camera feed plus measurement lines) is written to your device gallery. This app does not upload that photo or live frames.
  • Photos / gallery: Used only to write that AR still. The app does not read your existing photos.
  • Sensors / vibration: Used for tools such as spirit level, protractor, magnetic sensor, and haptic feedback.
  • Internet: Required to load AdMob interstitial ads when you open a tool. Tools themselves still run on the device. The app does not send your measurement values to our servers. The operating system or Google Play Services for AR may also use the network for their own updates.

Sharing

If you tap Share, content you choose is handed to the system share sheet. What happens next depends on the app you pick.

Liability

Readings are approximate. The software is provided “AS IS” without warranty. The publisher is not liable for reliance on readings. Full text: in-app Settings → Disclaimer, and DISCLAIMER.md in the project.

Children

Toolkit is a general-audience utility. It is not directed at children and is not designed for the Google Play Families policy program.
